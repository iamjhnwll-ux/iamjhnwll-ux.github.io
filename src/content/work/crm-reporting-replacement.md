## The goal

Replace a vendor CRM reporting module with an in-house solution on the same DealCloud data.

## The approach

- **Live data.** Power BI connects to live DealCloud data through DirectQuery.
- **Composite models.** Composite models are used alongside DirectQuery.
- **Executive dashboards.** Executives get real-time dashboards in Power BI.

## The result

- $25,000 saved by replacing the vendor module.
- Real-time dashboards for executives.
