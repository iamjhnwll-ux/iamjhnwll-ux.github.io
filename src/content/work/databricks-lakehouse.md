## The goal

Give business units one governed source for Power BI reporting, built from REST APIs, SharePoint, Excel and legacy CRM data.

## The approach

- **One ingestion layer.** I built a unified Databricks Lakehouse ingestion layer that consolidates REST API, SharePoint, Excel and legacy CRM data.
- **Investment analytics model.** I delivered a real estate investment Power BI semantic model with star and snowflake schemas and DAX for ROI, valuation trends and time intelligence.
- **Governance.** Workspace governance and row-level security support the client's GDPR and ISO 27001 requirements.

## The result

- One governed source for Power BI reporting across business units.
- A semantic model covering ROI, valuation trends and time intelligence.
