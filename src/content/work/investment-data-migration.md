## The goal

Move financial investment records from spreadsheets, Microsoft Lists, SharePoint and a legacy CRM into Argus Enterprise and DealCloud, with no data lost.

## The approach

- **Profiling.** The source data was profiled before migration.
- **Source-to-target mapping.** Source data was mapped to the target systems.
- **Cutover planning.** The switch to the new systems was planned.
- **Integrity checks.** Migrated data was checked for integrity.

## The result

- 30,000+ records migrated into Argus Enterprise and DealCloud.
- Zero data loss.
