## The goal

Take time out of recurring manual work, and make sure overdue client accounts are flagged.

## The approach

- **ETL/ELT automation (BMG Outsourcing).** I automated a recurring process with Python, Power Query (M) and Power Automate.
- **Client-notification pipeline (Emapta Global).** I built a Microsoft Fabric pipeline with Python and REST API integration that flags overdue accounts and sends targeted email alerts.
- **Finance reporting (Emapta Global).** I automated finance reporting with Power Query, VBA and Python, and delivered Fabric dashboards from Excel and ERP logistics and energy data.

## The result

- A recurring 4-hour process now takes under 20 minutes (92% less time).
- Fewer missed past-due incidents.
- Several hours saved per finance reporting cycle.
